privacy policy

At Kineto, we prioritize the responsible handling of your data. We want you to be fully informed about the information we gather, how it is utilized, and the steps we take to ensure your confidentiality remains intact. This transparency is designed to help you engage with our brand and products confidently.

This document outlines our procedures for collecting, storing, securing, and transmitting user data. We encourage you to review all sections to comprehend your rights and our dedication to safeguarding your sensitive personal information.

By accessing our services, you enter into a legally binding electronic agreement with Kineto under applicable laws, which is valid without the need for a physical or digital signature.

what information do we collect?

We gather details directly from you when you register an account on the Kineto platform. Additionally, we may automatically collect data through your devices and software, which includes:

  • Location details:
    Gathered via your device's GPS signals or surrounding Wi-Fi access points.
  • Device details:
    Information such as your hardware model, OS version, MAC and IP addresses, and device configurations. We may also track search queries and log-in activity to streamline your user experience.
  • Information from third-party sources:
    When you process online transactions (like placing an order), we capture necessary fulfillment details including billing addresses, payment methods, and contact profiles.

We only collect this data with your explicit prior consent. While you maintain the right to withhold this information, doing so may restrict your access to certain platform features.

how do we use your information?

The details you provide are utilized to elevate our service quality and enrich your overall experience. Specifically, we use your data for:

  • Facilitating user registration and account management.
  • Fulfilling product orders and service requests.
  • Delivering personalized web content tailored to your browsing history.
  • Presenting targeted advertising and promotional materials.

Furthermore, we analyze this data to evaluate market trends, gauge consumer preferences, and measure the performance of our products. This continuous analysis allows us to incrementally boost the value we deliver to our customers.

with whom do we share your information?

Protecting your intellectual property and personal data is our primary concern. We never distribute your information to external third parties without your direct authorization. However, to facilitate our operations, your details may be shared with:

  • Business partners & affiliates,
    who directly assist in delivering our products and services to you.
  • Other service providers,
    who manage payment gateways and ensure your ordering experience remains secure and uninterrupted.

In specific circumstances, identifiable information may be disclosed to authorized entities to comply with legal regulations or to defend Kineto's intellectual rights.

You retain control over how your data is used for promotional content and can adjust your ad-sharing preferences directly within your user account settings.

how do we keep your information secure?

We consistently upgrade our data security frameworks to prevent unauthorized access. Despite these robust measures, no digital transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as entirely infallible. We advise users to routinely monitor their accounts and report any anomalous activity immediately.

Your data is encrypted and housed on secure, centralized servers. You maintain the right to request access to, or modifications of, your stored information. While we strive to honor these requests, repetitive or excessive inquiries may incur a processing fee or be rejected if deemed unreasonable.

Please note that Kineto assumes no liability for the quality, accuracy, or privacy standards of services and content provided by external third parties linked through our platform.

use of cookies

Cookies are minor data files saved to your browser that help us track browsing habits and recognize returning visitors. These files are essential for delivering a highly personalized user experience across our services. Should you prefer not to utilize this feature, you may clear the cookies directly from your browser settings.

By navigating our website and utilizing our services, you automatically consent to the stipulations outlined in this Privacy Policy. Should you have any questions regarding our data handling practices, please reach out to our support team.
